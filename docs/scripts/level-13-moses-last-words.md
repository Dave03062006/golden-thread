# Level 13 script: *Moses' Last Words*

**Bible:** Deuteronomy 1; 6; 8; 18; 30–31; 34
**Age:** IV · The Covenant in the Desert
**Length:** about 35 minutes (5 episodes, then the quiz, then reflection and prayer)
**Status:** draft, waiting for Dave's approval.

## How to read this script

- Each **scene** is a checkpoint. Its numbered lines are the narration beats; the player taps to move from one to the next.
- *On screen* describes what the scene shows. It's the brief for the art in both styles.
- **[Q]** marks an exact quotation from the Bible. Its wording in both translations is in the verse table at the end. Everything else is our own retelling.
- **Play** is the episode's interactive moment. **Thread** is its golden thread. **Read** is the passage the game sends the player to.
- "LORD" in capitals stands for God's holy name, printed in small capitals in the RSV.

### What this level is about

Forty years after the Passover, God's people are camped on the plains of Moab, across the Jordan from Jericho. A whole new generation has grown up in the wilderness, and Moses, now a hundred and twenty years old, knows he will not cross the river with them. So, like a father before a long goodbye, he gathers everyone together and speaks to them one last time.

He tells them to remember the way God led them. He gives them the words that every Jewish family still prays each day: "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD; and you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart." He promises that God will send another prophet like him. He sets before them life and death, and begs them: "Choose life." Then he climbs Mount Nebo, and God shows him the whole promised land, and Moses dies there, in God's care.

Jesus answered the devil with Moses' words, called the "Hear, O Israel" the first of all the commandments, and was the prophet like Moses that God had promised. He is the life Moses told the people to choose. And on the mountain of the Transfiguration, Moses at last stood in the promised land, talking with him.

**How it's shown:** Moses' death is told gently, as the end of a long, full life. He is shown resting on the mountain at sunset, and then the people mourning him; his body is never shown.

### Play: remember the way
- **What it is:** courier routes (spec §13.7, kind 15).
- **How it plays:** a map of the whole journey, from Egypt to the plains of Moab. The player carries Moses' memory along the road, stop by stop (drag, or the arrow keys and Space): Egypt and the Passover, the Red Sea, the manna in the wilderness of Sin, Sinai and the commandments, water from the rock, the bronze serpent, and at last the plains of Moab. At each stop a picture lights up in the sky with a few words of what God did there. When the road is finished, the whole way shines.
- **Teaches:** the whole journey of the Exodus, Levels 7 to 12, as one story of God's care; and that remembering what God has done is the way to trust him for what is to come.
- **Gentle mode:** the memory moves along the road by itself; the player only taps to light each picture.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *"Man does not live by bread alone, but… by everything that proceeds out of the mouth of the LORD" → Jesus, forty days in the wilderness, answers the devil with Moses' words.* The glowing object is the manna on the ground.
- **[Q] Luke 4:1:** "And Jesus, full of the Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan, and was led by the Spirit"
- **[Q] Luke 4:2:** "for forty days in the wilderness, tempted by the devil. And he ate nothing in those days; and when they were ended, he was hungry."
- **[Q] Luke 4:4:** "And Jesus answered him, "It is written, 'Man shall not live by bread alone.'""
- **What it shows:** Israel spent forty years in the wilderness, learning to trust God for their daily bread. Jesus, the new Israel, spent forty days in the wilderness, and was tempted where they had failed. Hungry, he answered the devil with these very words of Moses. Every Lent, the Church keeps Jesus' forty days, fasting a little so as to remember that we live by God's word.
- **Source:** Luke 4:1–13; Matthew 4:1–11; Catechism 538–540, 2835.

**Where and when:**
- **Deuteronomy.** The name means "the second law," because Moses repeats the law to the new generation before they enter the land. It is written as Moses' farewell speeches, given in the last weeks of his life.
- **The plains of Moab.** The wide plain on the east bank of the Jordan, just north of the Dead Sea, across from Jericho. Israel camped there at the end of the forty years (Level 12).
- **"Your clothing did not wear out."** In forty years of walking, God cared even for their sandals (Deuteronomy 29:5).

**Read:** Deuteronomy 8:1–18.

## Episode 2 · "Hear, O Israel"

4. *(Play: on the doorposts.)*

### Scene 2.3 · When your son asks
*On screen:* Years later, in the promised land: a boy in a stone house asks his father a question, and the father takes him on his knee and tells him the whole story, as pictures of Egypt, the sea and the land pass by.

1. **[Q] Deuteronomy 6:20** ""When your son asks you in time to come, 'What is the meaning of the testimonies and the statutes and the ordinances which the LORD our God has commanded you?'"

3. **[Q] Deuteronomy 6:23** "and he brought us out from there, that he might bring us in and give us the land which he swore to give to our fathers."
4. **[Q] Deuteronomy 6:24** "And the LORD commanded us to do all these statutes, to fear the LORD our God, for our good always, that he might preserve us alive, as at this day."

### Play: on the doorposts
- **What it is:** memory (spec §13.7, kind 14).
- **How it plays:** the doorpost of a family's tent, and the words "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD; and you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your might." Some words are missing. One by one, the player chooses each missing word (Hear, one, love, heart, soul, might), and it is carved into the wood. When the words are complete, the little case of the *mezuzah* is set on the doorpost, and the family touch it as they go in.
- **Teaches:** the words of the *Shema*, by heart; and that God's word belongs everywhere in daily life.
- **Gentle mode:** the right word glows among the choices.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD; and you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart" → Jesus: "The first is, 'Hear, O Israel…'"* The glowing object is the words on the doorpost.
- **[Q] Mark 12:29:** "Jesus answered, "The first is, 'Hear, O Israel: The Lord our God, the Lord is one;"
- **[Q] Mark 12:30:** "and you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind, and with all your strength.'"
- **What it shows:** When a scribe asked Jesus which commandment was first of all, Jesus answered with the words of Moses that every Jewish child knew by heart, and joined to them a second: "You shall love your neighbor as yourself" (Level 11). Love of God with everything we have is the first and greatest commandment; the whole law hangs on it.
- **Source:** Mark 12:28–34; Matthew 22:34–40; Catechism 201–202, 2083.

**Where and when:**
- **The *mezuzah*.** Jewish families still fix a small case to the right-hand doorpost of their homes, holding a tiny scroll with the *Shema*, and many touch it as they go in and out.
- **Frontlets.** Small leather boxes with the words inside, worn on the forehead and the arm during prayer, called *tefillin* or phylacteries (Matthew 23:5).
- **"With all your might."** The Hebrew word means "very much": love God not a little, but completely.

**Read:** Deuteronomy 6.

## Episode 3 · A Prophet Like Moses

**Deuteronomy 18:15–19; 31:1–8, 23–24 · about 7 minutes · 3 scenes**

### Scene 3.1 · A prophet like me
*On screen:* Moses remembers Sinai: the mountain in fire and thunder, and the people standing far off, afraid, begging Moses to speak to them instead. Back on the plains, he looks out over the people and far beyond them, as if he could see someone coming.

1. **[Q] Deuteronomy 18:15** ""The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among you, from your brethren—him you shall heed—"
2. **[Q] Deuteronomy 18:16** "just as you desired of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, when you said, 'Let me not hear again the voice of the LORD my God, or see this great fire any more, lest I die.'"
3. **[Q] Deuteronomy 18:18** "I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brethren; and I will put my words in his mouth, and he shall speak to them all that I command him."
4. Through the centuries, many prophets came to Israel: Samuel, Elijah, Isaiah, Jeremiah. But the people kept waiting for the one prophet who would be truly like Moses, who would know God face to face.

### Scene 3.2 · "I am no longer able"
*On screen:* Moses, leaning on his staff, speaks to the whole people. He is old but his eyes are bright. Behind him, the Jordan, and the land beyond.

1. **[Q] Deuteronomy 31:7** "Then Moses summoned Joshua, and said to him in the sight of all Israel, "Be strong and of good courage; for you shall go with this people into the land which the LORD has sworn to their fathers to give them; and you shall put them in possession of it."

### Play: be strong and of good courage
- **What it is:** a quiet interaction (spec §13.7, kind 20).
- **How it plays:** Moses stands before Joshua with all Israel watching. The player rests Moses' hands on Joshua's head (press and hold), and while they rest there, the light passes from Moses to Joshua, and Moses' words rise one by one: "Be strong," "and of good courage," "the LORD goes before you," "he will be with you," "he will not fail you or forsake you." When the light has passed, Joshua stands, and the tribes turn one by one to face him (tap each to turn, or press Space).
- **Teaches:** that God's work goes on from one generation to the next; that courage comes from knowing God goes ahead of us.
- **Gentle mode:** a short hold is enough, and the tribes turn by themselves.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or Space.

**Thread:** *"The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me" → Peter: "Moses said, 'The Lord God will raise up for you a prophet…'" and the Father: "This is my beloved Son… listen to him."* The glowing object is Moses, looking far beyond the camp.
- **[Q] Acts 3:22:** "Moses said, 'The Lord God will raise up for you a prophet from your brethren as he raised me up. You shall listen to him in whatever he tells you."
- **[Q] Matthew 17:5:** "He was still speaking, when lo, a bright cloud overshadowed them, and a voice from the cloud said, "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased; listen to him.""
- **What it shows:** Moses promised that God would raise up another prophet like him, and told the people to listen to him. After Pentecost, Saint Peter told the crowds in Jerusalem that this prophet had come: Jesus, who did not just speak God's words, but is God's Word. At the Transfiguration, with Moses standing beside him, the Father's voice said of Jesus the very words of Moses' promise: "listen to him."
- **Source:** Acts 3:17–26; John 1:45; 6:14; Matthew 17:1–8; Catechism 1093, 2582.

**Where and when:**
- **A hundred and twenty years.** Moses lived forty years in Egypt as a prince, forty in Midian as a shepherd, and forty leading Israel in the wilderness (Acts 7:23, 30, 36).
- **Joshua.** Moses' helper since the battle with Amalek (Level 9), one of the two faithful spies (Level 12), and now the leader who will bring the people into the land (Level 14).
- **The book of the law.** Moses wrote the law in a book and gave it to the priests, to be kept beside the ark of the covenant and read aloud to all the people every seven years (Deuteronomy 31:9–13, 26).

**Read:** Deuteronomy 18:15–19; 31:1–8, 23–29.

### Play: choose life
- **What it is:** matching and naming (spec §13.7, kind 10).
- **How it plays:** the two roads, side by side. Things a person can choose come one by one: loving the LORD, walking in his ways, keeping his commandments, listening to his voice, holding fast to him; and turning away, serving other gods, forgetting what he has done. The player sends each to the road it belongs to (tap, or the arrow keys and Space). Each one sent to the road of life lights another stretch of it, until it shines all the way across the river; the road away stays dry and empty.
- **Teaches:** that God sets the choice before us, every day; and what choosing life looks like.
- **Gentle mode:** the right road glows.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *"I have set before you life and death… therefore choose life" → Jesus: "I came that they may have life, and have it abundantly," and "I am the way, and the truth, and the life."* The glowing object is the road of life, across the river.
- **[Q] John 10:10:** "The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy; I came that they may have life, and have it abundantly."
- **[Q] John 14:6:** "Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life; no one comes to the Father, but by me."
- **What it shows:** Moses set life before the people as a road to walk: loving God, obeying his voice, holding fast to him. Jesus is that road, and that life: "I am the way, and the truth, and the life." To choose life is to choose him, and to follow him, every day.
- **Source:** John 10:10; 14:6; Matthew 7:13–14; Catechism 1696, 2057.

**Where and when:**
- **"Heaven and earth to witness."** Covenants in the ancient world were sworn before witnesses. Moses calls on the whole of creation to witness the people's choice.
- **Not in heaven, nor beyond the sea.** Saint Paul quotes these verses to say that the word is near us in Christ: "if you confess with your lips that Jesus is Lord… you will be saved" (Romans 10:6–9).
- **The two ways.** One of the oldest Christian writings, the *Didache* (about AD 100), begins: "There are two ways, one of life and one of death."

**Read:** Deuteronomy 30:11–20.

## Episode 5 · Moses Sees the Land

**Deuteronomy 34:1–12 · about 8 minutes · 3 scenes**

### Scene 5.1 · From the top of Pisgah
*On screen:* Moses climbs Mount Nebo alone, slowly, with his staff. At the top, in the evening light, the whole promised land is spread out before him: the hills of Gilead, the far north, the green middle of the land, the hills of Judah down to the western sea, the dry south, and right below, the valley of Jericho with its palm trees. *(Play: all the land.)*

3. **[Q] Deuteronomy 34:3** "the Negeb, and the Plain, that is, the valley of Jericho the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar."
4. *(Play: all the land.)*
5. **[Q] Deuteronomy 34:4** "And the LORD said to him, "This is the land of which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, 'I will give it to your descendants.' I have let you see it with your eyes, but you shall not go over there.""

*On screen:* Sunset. Moses sits on the top of the mountain, his staff across his knees, looking at the land. The light fades slowly, and the stars come out. Down on the plains, the people stand in silence; then they weep.

### Scene 5.3 · Face to face
*On screen:* Morning on the plains. Joshua stands before the people, and they turn to him. Then the picture of Moses, as a young shepherd at the burning bush, as a leader at the sea, on Sinai with the tablets, and as an old man on Nebo, glows in the sky and fades into the dawn.

### Play: all the land
- **What it is:** light and reveal (spec §13.7, kind 11).
- **How it plays:** the view from the top of Nebo, at first in evening haze. As God shows Moses the land, the player lights each part of it in turn (tap, or the arrow keys and Space): Gilead, as far as Dan; Naphtali; Ephraim and Manasseh; Judah, as far as the western sea; the Negeb; and the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar. Each glows golden and its name appears, until the whole land shines below him.
- **Teaches:** the promised land, as God showed it to Moses; and that God kept his promise to Abraham, Isaac and Jacob.
- **Gentle mode:** the next part of the land glows, and lights itself after a moment.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *Moses sees the land but does not go in → on the mountain of the Transfiguration, Moses stands in the promised land at last, talking with Jesus.* The glowing object is the land, shining below the mountain.
- **[Q] Matthew 17:1:** "And after six days Jesus took with him Peter and James and John his brother, and led them up a high mountain apart."
- **[Q] Matthew 17:3:** "And behold, there appeared to them Moses and Elijah, talking with him."
- **What it shows:** Moses died looking at the land he could not enter. But that was not the end of his story. Centuries later, on a high mountain in that very land, Moses appeared with Elijah, talking with Jesus in his glory. God had brought him into the promised land after all, and into something far greater: the life of heaven, which Jesus opened to all who belong to him.
- **Source:** Matthew 17:1–8; Luke 9:28–36; Catechism 554–556, 1023.

**Where and when:**
- **Mount Nebo.** A ridge in Jordan, about 700 meters high, looking out over the Dead Sea and the Jordan valley. On a clear day you can see Jericho, Bethlehem and the Mount of Olives. A church stands on the top today, and Saint John Paul II prayed there in the year 2000.
- **Pisgah.** The name of the top of the ridge.
- **"No man knows the place of his burial."** Moses' grave was never found. The letter of Jude mentions an old tradition that the archangel Michael argued with the devil over Moses' body (Jude 1:9).
- **The end of the Torah.** Deuteronomy is the last of the five books of Moses, the Torah. The story goes on in the book of Joshua (Level 14).

**Read:** Deuteronomy 34.
