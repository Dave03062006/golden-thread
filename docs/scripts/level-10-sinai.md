# Level 10 script: *Sinai*

**Bible:** Exodus 19–20; 24; 31:18–34:35
**Age:** IV · The Covenant in the Desert
**Length:** about 50 minutes (6 episodes, then the quiz, then reflection and prayer)
**Status:** draft, waiting for Dave's approval.

## How to read this script

- Each **scene** is a checkpoint. Its numbered lines are the narration beats; the player taps to move from one to the next.
- *On screen* describes what the scene shows. It's the brief for the art in both styles.
- **[Q]** marks an exact quotation from the Bible. Its wording in both translations is in the verse table at the end. Everything else is our own retelling.
- **Play** is the episode's interactive moment. **Thread** is its golden thread. **Read** is the passage the game sends the player to.
- "LORD" in capitals stands for God's holy name, printed in small capitals in the RSV.

### What this level is about

Three months after leaving Egypt, God's people camp before Mount Sinai. God calls them to be his own people, and comes down on the mountain in fire, cloud and thunder. He gives them the Ten Commandments, and makes a covenant with them, sealed with blood: "All that the LORD has spoken we will do." Then, while Moses is on the mountain for forty days, the people make a golden calf and worship it. Moses breaks the tablets of stone, and pleads with God for his people, and God forgives them. He speaks with Moses face to face, as a friend, and shows him his glory: "The LORD, the LORD, a God merciful and gracious."

The covenant at Sinai is the heart of the Old Testament. The level shows that God's commandments are a gift to a people he has already set free, that sin breaks the covenant but God's mercy is greater, and that all of it points to Jesus: the new covenant in his blood, the commandments fulfilled in love, and the glory of God seen at last in his face.

## Episode 1 · The Mountain of Fire

**Exodus 19 · about 8 minutes · 3 scenes**

### Scene 1.1 · Before the mountain
*On screen:* The wilderness of Sinai. The camp spread out on a plain before a great mountain of red granite. Moses walks up the mountain alone; above, an eagle circles.

1. Three months after they left Egypt, God's people came to the mountain where God had first spoken to Moses from the burning bush.
2. **[Q] Exodus 19:2** "And when they set out from Rephidim and came into the wilderness of Sinai, they encamped in the wilderness; and there Israel encamped before the mountain."
3. **[Q] Exodus 19:4** "You have seen what I did to the Egyptians, and how I bore you on eagles' wings and brought you to myself."
4. **[Q] Exodus 19:5** "Now therefore, if you will obey my voice and keep my covenant, you shall be my own possession among all peoples; for all the earth is mine,"
5. **[Q] Exodus 19:6** "and you shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation. These are the words which you shall speak to the children of Israel.""

### Scene 1.2 · Up and down the mountain
*On screen:* Moses comes down to the elders and the people, then climbs again, carrying God's words down and the people's answer up. The people wash their garments by the tents. *(Play: up and down the mountain.)*

1. **[Q] Exodus 19:7** "So Moses came and called the elders of the people, and set before them all these words which the LORD had commanded him."
2. **[Q] Exodus 19:8** "And all the people answered together and said, "All that the LORD has spoken we will do." And Moses reported the words of the people to the LORD."
3. *(Play: up and down the mountain.)*
4. **[Q] Exodus 19:10** "And the LORD said to Moses, "Go to the people and consecrate them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments,"
5. **[Q] Exodus 19:11** "and be ready by the third day; for on the third day the LORD will come down upon Mount Sinai in the sight of all the people."

### Scene 1.3 · The third day
*On screen:* Morning of the third day. Thunder and lightning; a thick cloud on the mountain; smoke rising like a kiln; the mountain shaking. The people stand trembling at the foot of the mountain, at the boundary Moses has set. Moses climbs into the cloud.

1. **[Q] Exodus 19:16** "On the morning of the third day there were thunders and lightnings, and a thick cloud upon the mountain, and a very loud trumpet blast, so that all the people who were in the camp trembled."
2. **[Q] Exodus 19:18** "And Mount Sinai was wrapped in smoke, because the LORD descended upon it in fire; and the smoke of it went up like the smoke of a kiln, and the whole mountain quaked greatly."
3. **[Q] Exodus 19:19** "And as the sound of the trumpet grew louder and louder, Moses spoke, and God answered him in thunder."
4. **[Q] Exodus 19:20** "And the LORD came down upon Mount Sinai, to the top of the mountain; and the LORD called Moses to the top of the mountain, and Moses went up."

### Play: up and down the mountain
- **What it is:** courier routes (spec §13.7, kind 15).
- **How it plays:** the mountain in profile, with a winding path from the camp to the top. The player takes Moses up and down it, carrying the words between God and his people: up to hear God's word; down to tell it to the elders; up again with the people's answer, "All that the LORD has spoken we will do"; and down with God's word to get ready. Each turn of the path is a stop: tap the next one (or press Space), and Moses climbs or comes down to it. At the end of each journey, the words he carries are shown.
- **Teaches:** Moses as the go-between of God and his people; that God speaks first, and his people answer.
- **Gentle mode:** Moses walks the path by himself.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, the arrow keys, or Space for the next stop.

**Thread:** *"You shall be to me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation" → "You are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation."* The glowing object is the camp at the foot of the mountain.
- **[Q] 1 Peter 2:9:** "But you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, God's own people, that you may declare the wonderful deeds of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light."
- **[Q] 1 Peter 2:10:** "Once you were no people but now you are God's people; once you had not received mercy but now you have received mercy."
- **What it shows:** At Sinai, God called the people he had set free to be his own: a holy nation, a kingdom of priests, to bring the whole earth to him. Saint Peter gives the same words to everyone baptized into Christ. Every Christian belongs to God's people, shares in Christ's priesthood, and is sent to tell what God has done.
- **Source:** 1 Peter 2:4–10; Revelation 1:6; Catechism 709, 1268, 1546.

**Where and when:**
- **Mount Sinai.** Also called Horeb. Tradition places it at Jebel Musa, "the mountain of Moses," in the south of the Sinai peninsula, 2,285 meters high. At its foot stands Saint Catherine's Monastery, one of the oldest monasteries in the world.
- **On eagles' wings.** An eagle teaches its young to fly, and swoops beneath them when they fall. Deuteronomy 32:11 uses the same picture for the way God carried his people out of Egypt.
- **The third month.** Jewish tradition keeps the giving of the Law at the feast of Weeks, fifty days after Passover. Christians keep Pentecost on that same feast, fifty days after Easter.

**Read:** Exodus 19.

## Episode 2 · The Ten Commandments

**Exodus 20:1–21 · about 9 minutes · 3 scenes**

### Scene 2.1 · "I am the LORD your God"
*On screen:* The mountain in cloud and fire. God's words sound out over the camp; the people listen, afraid. On the mountainside, two great tablets of stone begin to glow.

1. **[Q] Exodus 20:1** "And God spoke all these words, saying,"
2. **[Q] Exodus 20:2** ""I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage."
3. God spoke first of what he had done. He had set his people free. His commandments were for a free people, to keep them free.
4. **[Q] Exodus 20:3** ""You shall have no other gods before me."
5. **[Q] Exodus 20:7** ""You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless who takes his name in vain."
6. **[Q] Exodus 20:8** ""Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy."

### Scene 2.2 · Love of neighbor
*On screen:* The camp: families, old and young, neighbors at their tents, as the words go on.

1. **[Q] Exodus 20:12** ""Honor your father and your mother, that your days may be long in the land which the LORD your God gives you."
2. **[Q] Exodus 20:13** ""You shall not kill."
3. **[Q] Exodus 20:14** ""You shall not commit adultery."
4. **[Q] Exodus 20:15** ""You shall not steal."
5. **[Q] Exodus 20:16** ""You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor."
6. **[Q] Exodus 20:17** ""You shall not covet your neighbor's house; you shall not covet your neighbor's wife, or his manservant, or his maidservant, or his ox, or his ass, or anything that is your neighbor's.""

### Scene 2.3 · The people stood afar off
*On screen:* The two tablets of stone, five words carved on each. Then the people, afraid, step back from the mountain, while Moses walks toward the thick darkness where God is. *(Play: the ten words.)*

1. Ten words God spoke: the first three about loving God, the other seven about loving our neighbor.
2. *(Play: the ten words.)*
3. **[Q] Exodus 20:18** "Now when all the people perceived the thunderings and the lightnings and the sound of the trumpet and the mountain smoking, the people were afraid and trembled; and they stood afar off,"
4. **[Q] Exodus 20:20** "And Moses said to the people, "Do not fear; for God has come to prove you, and that the fear of him may be before your eyes, that you may not sin.""
5. **[Q] Exodus 20:21** "And the people stood afar off, while Moses drew near to the thick darkness where God was."

### Play: the ten words
- **What it is:** memory (spec §13.7, kind 14; the spec's own example).
- **How it plays:** two tablets of stone, with ten places to carve. One by one, the player recalls the commandments in order: for each place, the first, the second and so on, three short forms are offered, and the player chooses the one that belongs there (tap, or the arrow keys and Space). Each one chosen is carved into the stone. The short forms are the ones the Catechism gives: "I am the LORD your God: you shall not have strange gods before me," "You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain," "Remember to keep holy the LORD's Day," "Honor your father and your mother," "You shall not kill," "You shall not commit adultery," "You shall not steal," "You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor," "You shall not covet your neighbor's wife," "You shall not covet your neighbor's goods." A wrong choice gets a gentle "not yet." When all ten are carved, the tablets shine.
- **Teaches:** the Ten Commandments, in the order the Church teaches them; the first three about God, the last seven about our neighbor.
- **Gentle mode:** the right one glows.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *The Ten Commandments → Jesus: "You shall love the Lord your God… You shall love your neighbor as yourself."* The glowing object is the two tablets of stone.
- **[Q] Matthew 22:37:** "And he said to him, "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind."
- **[Q] Matthew 22:39:** "And a second is like it, You shall love your neighbor as yourself."
- **[Q] Matthew 22:40:** "On these two commandments depend all the law and the prophets.""
- **What it shows:** When Jesus was asked which commandment was the greatest, he gathered all ten into two: love God with everything you have, and love your neighbor as yourself. The first tablet is love of God; the second is love of neighbor. Jesus did not take the commandments away. He came to fulfill them, and to give us the grace to keep them, out of love.
- **Source:** Matthew 22:34–40; Matthew 5:17; John 14:15; Catechism 2055, 2067.

**Where and when:**
- **The "ten words."** The Bible calls the commandments "the ten words" (Exodus 34:28). The Catholic Church numbers them as Saint Augustine did, with two about coveting at the end.
- **Two tablets.** Tablets of stone, written "with the finger of God" (Exodus 31:18). Treaties of the time were written out twice, one copy for each side.
- **The sabbath and the Lord's Day.** Christians keep Sunday holy, the day Jesus rose, as the Lord's Day.

**Read:** Exodus 20:1–21.

## Episode 3 · The Blood of the Covenant

**Exodus 24 · about 8 minutes · 3 scenes**

### Scene 3.1 · Twelve pillars
*On screen:* Early morning at the foot of the mountain. Moses builds an altar of stones, and around it the people set up twelve standing stones, one for each tribe. *(Play: twelve pillars.)*

1. **[Q] Exodus 24:3** "Moses came and told the people all the words of the LORD and all the ordinances; and all the people answered with one voice, and said, "All the words which the LORD has spoken we will do.""
2. **[Q] Exodus 24:4** "And Moses wrote all the words of the LORD. And he rose early in the morning, and built an altar at the foot of the mountain, and twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes of Israel."
3. *(Play: twelve pillars.)*
4. **[Q] Exodus 24:5** "And he sent young men of the people of Israel, who offered burnt offerings and sacrificed peace offerings of oxen to the LORD."

### Scene 3.2 · The blood of the covenant
*On screen:* Moses stands by the altar with the book of the covenant open in his hands and reads to all the people. Then he takes a branch, dips it in a basin, and sprinkles the people.

1. **[Q] Exodus 24:6** "And Moses took half of the blood and put it in basins, and half of the blood he threw against the altar."
2. **[Q] Exodus 24:7** "Then he took the book of the covenant, and read it in the hearing of the people; and they said, "All that the LORD has spoken we will do, and we will be obedient.""
3. **[Q] Exodus 24:8** "And Moses took the blood and threw it upon the people, and said, "Behold the blood of the covenant which the LORD has made with you in accordance with all these words.""

### Scene 3.3 · They beheld God
*On screen:* High on the mountain, Moses, Aaron and seventy elders. Beneath them, a pavement like blue sapphire, clear as the sky. They eat and drink before God. Then Moses climbs on alone into the cloud, where the glory of the LORD burns like fire.

1. **[Q] Exodus 24:9** "Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel went up,"
2. **[Q] Exodus 24:10** "and they saw the God of Israel; and there was under his feet as it were a pavement of sapphire stone, like the very heaven for clearness."
3. **[Q] Exodus 24:11** "And he did not lay his hand on the chief men of the people of Israel; they beheld God, and ate and drank."
4. **[Q] Exodus 24:18** "And Moses entered the cloud, and went up on the mountain. And Moses was on the mountain forty days and forty nights."

### Play: twelve pillars
- **What it is:** building and stacking (spec §13.7, kind 3).
- **How it plays:** around the altar at the foot of the mountain are twelve places, and the player sets up a standing stone on each (tap the next place, or press Space). Each stone is named for one of the twelve tribes, the sons of Jacob from Level 4: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Joseph and Benjamin. When all twelve stand, the whole people is gathered around the altar.
- **Teaches:** that the covenant is made with the whole people, every tribe; the names of the twelve tribes.
- **Gentle mode:** a tap anywhere sets the next stone.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or Space.

**Thread:** *"Behold the blood of the covenant" → Jesus at the Last Supper: "This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many."* The glowing object is the basin of blood beside the altar.
- **[Q] Mark 14:23:** "And he took a cup, and when he had given thanks he gave it to them, and they all drank of it."
- **[Q] Mark 14:24:** "And he said to them, "This is my blood of the covenant, which is poured out for many."
- **What it shows:** At Sinai, Moses sealed the covenant between God and his people with blood, and then the elders ate and drank in God's presence. At the Last Supper, Jesus used Moses' very words over the cup: "my blood of the covenant." The new covenant is sealed not with the blood of oxen, but with Jesus' own blood, and at every Mass his people eat and drink in the presence of God.
- **Source:** Mark 14:22–25; Hebrews 9:18–22; Hebrews 12:24; Catechism 610, 1339, 1365.

**Where and when:**
- **A covenant.** A solemn promise binding two sides together, like a marriage or a treaty. God promises to be their God; the people promise to keep his word.
- **Blood.** In the Bible, blood stands for life. Sprinkled on the altar (God's side) and on the people, it bound them together in one life.
- **Forty days and forty nights.** Moses stays on the mountain forty days. Elijah will walk forty days to the same mountain (1 Kings 19:8), and Jesus will fast forty days in the desert.

**Read:** Exodus 24.

## Episode 4 · The Golden Calf

**Exodus 31:18–32:20 · about 9 minutes · 4 scenes**

### Scene 4.1 · "Make us gods"
*On screen:* The camp at the foot of the mountain, the top still hidden in cloud. Restless people crowd around Aaron. They take off their gold earrings and pile them up.

1. Moses was gone a long time, forty days. Down in the camp, the people grew restless.
2. **[Q] Exodus 32:1** "When the people saw that Moses delayed to come down from the mountain, the people gathered themselves together to Aaron, and said to him, "Up, make us gods, who shall go before us; as for this Moses, the man who brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.""
3. **[Q] Exodus 32:3** "So all the people took off the rings of gold which were in their ears, and brought them to Aaron."
4. **[Q] Exodus 32:4** "And he received the gold at their hand, and fashioned it with a graving tool, and made a molten calf; and they said, "These are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt!""

### Scene 4.2 · A feast before the calf
*On screen:* A golden calf on an altar of stones in the middle of the camp. The people feast and dance around it. *(Play: the true words and the twisted.)*

1. **[Q] Exodus 32:5** "When Aaron saw this, he built an altar before it; and Aaron made proclamation and said, "Tomorrow shall be a feast to the LORD.""
2. **[Q] Exodus 32:6** "And they rose up early on the morrow, and offered burnt offerings and brought peace offerings; and the people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play."
3. *(Play: the true words and the twisted.)*

### Scene 4.3 · Two tablets of stone
*On screen:* High on the mountain, Moses receives the two tablets, glowing with God's writing. Then he turns and starts down, Joshua waiting for him on the way.

1. **[Q] Exodus 31:18** "And he gave to Moses, when he had made an end of speaking with him upon Mount Sinai, the two tables of the testimony, tables of stone, written with the finger of God."
2. **[Q] Exodus 32:7** "And the LORD said to Moses, "Go down; for your people, whom you brought up out of the land of Egypt, have corrupted themselves;"
3. **[Q] Exodus 32:15** "And Moses turned, and went down from the mountain with the two tables of the testimony in his hands, tables that were written on both sides; on the one side and on the other were they written."
4. **[Q] Exodus 32:16** "And the tables were the work of God, and the writing was the writing of God, graven upon the tables."

### Scene 4.4 · The broken tablets
*On screen:* Moses and Joshua on the slope above the camp. Below, the dancing around the calf. Moses lifts the tablets and throws them down, and they break on the rocks at the foot of the mountain. The calf is thrown into the fire.

1. **[Q] Exodus 32:17** "When Joshua heard the noise of the people as they shouted, he said to Moses, "There is a noise of war in the camp.""
2. **[Q] Exodus 32:18** "But he said, "It is not the sound of shouting for victory, or the sound of the cry of defeat, but the sound of singing that I hear.""
3. **[Q] Exodus 32:19** "And as soon as he came near the camp and saw the calf and the dancing, Moses' anger burned hot, and he threw the tables out of his hands and broke them at the foot of the mountain."
4. **[Q] Exodus 32:20** "And he took the calf which they had made, and burnt it with fire, and ground it to powder, and scattered it upon the water, and made the people of Israel drink it."
5. Only forty days before, they had promised, "All that the LORD has spoken we will do." Now they had broken the covenant, as the tablets lay broken on the ground.

### Play: the true words and the twisted
- **What it is:** spot the twist (spec §13.7, kind 20), as in Level 1's "spot the twist."
- **How it plays:** two passages side by side. On the left, what God said from the mountain: "I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. You shall have no other gods before me." On the right, what the people said before the calf: "These are your gods, O Israel, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt!" and Aaron's "Tomorrow shall be a feast to the LORD." The player taps the words on the right that twist God's word. There are three: "These are your gods" (a calf of gold, made by their own hands, in place of the one God); "who brought you up out of the land of Egypt" (giving the calf the glory of what God had done); and "a feast to the LORD" (calling a feast for an idol by God's holy name). Each twist found is explained.
- **Teaches:** how idolatry steals God's glory and gives it to something we have made; how close the false can sound to the true.
- **Gentle mode:** the twisted words are faintly underlined.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *The golden calf → "Do not be idolaters as some of them were," and Jesus: "You shall worship the Lord your God and him only shall you serve."* The glowing object is the broken tablets at the foot of the mountain.
- **[Q] 1 Corinthians 10:7:** "Do not be idolaters as some of them were; as it is written, "The people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to dance.""
- **[Q] Matthew 4:10:** "Then Jesus said to him, "Begone, Satan! for it is written, 'You shall worship the Lord your God and him only shall you serve.'""
- **What it shows:** Saint Paul looks back at the golden calf and warns Christians: do not be idolaters as they were. An idol is anything we put in God's place: money, success, pleasure, what others think of us. When the devil tempted Jesus in the desert, Jesus answered with God's own word: worship God, and him alone.
- **Source:** 1 Corinthians 10:6–14; Matthew 4:8–10; Catechism 2112–2114.

**Where and when:**
- **A calf.** In Egypt and Canaan, the bull stood for strength, and gods were often shown standing on one. The people may have meant the calf to stand for the LORD himself, which was still forbidden: "You shall not make for yourself a graven image" (Exodus 20:4).
- **Rings of gold.** The Egyptians had given the people gold and silver as they left (Exodus 12:35). They now gave it for an idol.
- **Aaron.** Moses' brother, the one who should have stood firm, gave way to the crowd.

**Read:** Exodus 32:1–20.

*On screen:* High on the mountain in the cloud, Moses on his knees, his hands lifted up. *(Play: Moses pleads.)*

2. **[Q] Exodus 32:10** "now therefore let me alone, that my wrath may burn hot against them and I may consume them; but of you I will make a great nation.""
3. *(Play: Moses pleads.)*
4. **[Q] Exodus 32:14** "And the LORD repented of the evil which he thought to do to his people."

### Scene 5.2 · "Blot me out"
*On screen:* The next morning. Moses before the people, then climbing the mountain again, alone, to pray for them.

1. **[Q] Exodus 32:30** "On the morrow Moses said to the people, "You have sinned a great sin. And now I will go up to the LORD; perhaps I can make atonement for your sin.""

*On screen:* A tent pitched outside the camp. Moses walks to it; the people stand at their tent doors to watch. The pillar of cloud comes down and stands at the door of the tent, and all the people bow down where they stand.

1. **[Q] Exodus 33:7** "Now Moses used to take the tent and pitch it outside the camp, far off from the camp; and he called it the tent of meeting. And every one who sought the LORD would go out to the tent of meeting, which was outside the camp."

3. **[Q] Exodus 33:10** "And when all the people saw the pillar of cloud standing at the door of the tent, all the people would rise up and worship, every man at his tent door."

5. **[Q] Exodus 33:14** "And he said, "My presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.""

### Play: Moses pleads
- **What it is:** matching and naming (spec §13.7, kind 10).
- **How it plays:** Moses prays for his people in three pleas (Exodus 32:11–13). Each plea is shown in Moses' own words, and the player matches it to what it asks God to remember (tap, or the arrow keys and Space): "Why does thy wrath burn hot against thy people, whom thou hast brought forth out of the land of Egypt?" (they are your own people, whom you saved); "Why should the Egyptians say, 'With evil intent did he bring them forth'?" (your holy name, among the nations); "Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, thy servants" (your promise to their fathers). With each plea matched, the cloud on the mountain grows gentler, until God turns from his anger.
- **Teaches:** how to pray for others; that Moses' prayer rests on who God is and what he has promised.
- **Gentle mode:** the right match glows.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, or the arrow keys and Space.

**Thread:** *"The LORD used to speak to Moses face to face, as a man speaks to his friend" → Jesus: "I have called you friends."* The glowing object is the pillar of cloud at the door of the tent of meeting.
- **[Q] John 15:14:** "You are my friends if you do what I command you."
- **[Q] John 15:15:** "No longer do I call you servants, for the servant does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, for all that I have heard from my Father I have made known to you."
- **What it shows:** God spoke with Moses as a friend, and Moses prayed for his people as only a friend of God could. Jesus calls all his disciples his friends, and tells them everything he has heard from the Father. And as Moses offered to be blotted out for his people, Jesus gave his own life for us, and now "always lives to make intercession" for us (Hebrews 7:25).
- **Source:** John 15:12–15; Hebrews 7:25; Catechism 2574–2577, 2665.

**Where and when:**
- **A stiff-necked people.** Like an ox that will not bend its neck to the yoke: stubborn, unwilling to be led.
- **"The LORD repented."** The Bible speaks of God in human words. God does not change his mind as we do; Moses' prayer is part of the way God shows his mercy.
- **The tent of meeting.** A tent outside the camp where Moses met with God, before the tabernacle was built (Level 11).

**Read:** Exodus 32:7–14, 30–34; 33:7–17.

## Episode 6 · God's Glory Passes By

1. **[Q] Exodus 33:18** "Moses said, "I pray thee, show me thy glory.""

### Play: the LORD passed by
- **What it is:** light and reveal (spec §13.7, kind 11).
- **How it plays:** Moses stands in the cleft of the rock, covered by God's hand. God's glory passes by as a great light moving across the mountain, and the player follows it (drag, or the arrow keys). Wherever the light passes over the rock face, the words of God's name shine out, one after another: "merciful," "gracious," "slow to anger," "abounding in steadfast love," "and faithfulness," "keeping steadfast love for thousands," "forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin." When all are shown, the light has passed, and the whole rock glows.
- **Teaches:** who God says he is: above all, merciful.
- **Gentle mode:** the light moves by itself, and every word it passes shines.
- **Accessibility:** a pointer, the arrow keys, or Space to bring the light to the next word.

**Thread:** *"You cannot see my face" → "No one has ever seen God; the only Son… has made him known."* The glowing object is Moses' shining face.
- **[Q] John 1:14:** "And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, full of grace and truth; we have beheld his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father."
- **[Q] John 1:17:** "For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ."
- **[Q] John 1:18:** "No one has ever seen God; the only Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, he has made him known."
- **What it shows:** Moses asked to see God's glory, and saw only his back, as he passed. His face shone just from being near God. In Jesus, God's glory is seen at last in a human face: "we have beheld his glory." The God who called himself "merciful and gracious… abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness" came to live among us, "full of grace and truth."
- **Source:** John 1:14–18; 2 Corinthians 3:18; 2 Corinthians 4:6; Catechism 210–211.

**Where and when:**
- **The name of the LORD.** These words (Exodus 34:6–7) are repeated again and again in the Bible, in the Psalms and the prophets: they are how Israel remembered who God is.
- **Moses' horns.** In Latin, Saint Jerome's word for Moses' shining face could also mean "horned," so many old paintings and statues, like Michelangelo's Moses in Rome, show him with horns.
- **The veil.** Saint Paul writes that when we turn to the Lord the veil is taken away, and we all reflect his glory (2 Corinthians 3:16–18).

**Read:** Exodus 33:12–23; 34:1–9, 27–35.
